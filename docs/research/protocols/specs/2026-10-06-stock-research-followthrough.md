# Stock research and product follow-through

User intent: continue after either a passing or failing research result. Preserve
the accepted Q6 result and all closed negative studies; finish useful authorized
work without reopening budgets or turning implementation checks into speed claims.

## Research gate

Candidate: explicitly enable flash attention to obtain a supported fused decode
path beyond the tuned pristine stock baseline. Before quality or performance
collection, require a distinct useful mechanism. Inspect exact upstream
`a7312ae94f801fc9c6786dc56e38df57b964f697`, bind defaults, automatic support
resolution and the existing launch arguments. This is source feasibility, not
a new native experiment or an empirical quality/performance result.

Pass branch: only a distinct useful mechanism permits drafting a separate
quality provider and native registration. That registration must pin model,
runtime, workload, corpus, task items, quality tolerance and uncertainty gates,
matched tuned stock, fixed native budget, fresh product/consumer acceptance and
first-failure stop. No performance call is authorized by a source-only pass.

Fail branch: retain the mechanism rejection and continue the bounded stock
prototype. No switch to a different attention mode, candidate sweep, new
placement or approximate policy is inferred from this failed candidate.

## Product deliverable on the fail branch

Provide a checkout helper, `scripts/stock_product_status.py`, that makes the
qualified result and four neutral cases readable and runs the existing public
read-only validators. It must not edit the 63 wider or 41 original frozen files,
add controls, launch a model, publish a plan, write into native study roots or
replace the consolidated public CLI. The helper delegates to that CLI.

`status` reads a pinned catalogue of the two authoritative reports and the
bound transfer report. It presents one Q6 held-out result and four separate
wider outcomes, native counts, intervals and default selection. Its output
always says archived results, not fresh reconstruction. Missing, mismatched,
malformed or nonfinite evidence stops with exit 2; no optimistic substitute.

`verify --output-dir PATH` requires a fresh directory outside both native study
roots. It runs only `expertflow stock repeatability validate` and
`expertflow stock coverage validate`, using the current matching checkout.
Capture stdout/stderr, exit codes and wall costs. A fresh verification claim
requires both CLI decisions to verify evidence, expected statuses and unchanged
report/source/native-start digests before and after. No retry or native action.
On failure retain logs and say unverified. Verification is local reconstruction,
not a new timing result, portability test or customer acceptance.

## Scope and completion

The current artifact is a research checkout prototype, not a universal or
globally optimal compiler. Q6 utility is limited to the two declared workloads,
host/build and timing contract. Q4/Granite compatibility stays separate from
defaults gain. The quality-bounded provider is not implemented or qualified.

Complete source feasibility, helper tests, one independent review, live read-only
qualification, documentation and an executable usability handoff. Then the
remaining product gate is actual independent-user workflow evidence: this agent
can prepare the procedure and demonstrate commands, but cannot invent users,
demand, task completion or launch acceptance. No merge, push or publication.
